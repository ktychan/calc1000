\begin{document} \section{Derivatives of trigonometric functions}
All derivatives of trig functions come from \[\frac{d}{dx}\sin(x) = \cos(x) \quad\text{and}\quad \frac{d}{dx} \cos(x) = -\sin(x)\] which in turn come from the first two special limits on page~\pageref{eg:limit-change-of-variable}.

Derivatives of all other trig functions \(\tan(x), \cot(x), \sec(x), \csc(x)\) can be calculated from the derivatives of \(\sin(x)\) and \(\cos(x)\) using basic differentiation rules. We will calculate derivatives of inverse trig functions next week.
