\begin{document} \section{Integration Strategies}

\textbf{Study skills}.  How you study matters.
Rote practice without understanding how to apply definitions and theorems is ineffective for university courses.
Practise active recall and mindfully associate each question with relevant concepts and techniques.
Remember that \hlmain{definitions and theorems guarantee correctness}.

We further develop integration skills by tackling the complexity of deciding if a substitution is required.  Before starting, \hlwarn{recall} the strategy for choosing a substitution from last week.

\begin{exercise} \label{ex:choose-substitution}
  Evaluate the following integrals.

  \begin{multicols}{3}
    \begin{enumerate}[wide, label=(\alph*)]
      % \item \(\int_{-1}^{2} x(x^{2} + 1) \;dx\)
      \item \(\int_{-1}^{0} \frac{1}{x^{2} + 1} \;dx\)
      \item \(\int_{0}^{1} \frac{x}{x^{2} + 1} \;dx\)
      \item \(\int_{1}^{2} \frac{x^{2} + 1}{x} \;dx\)
      % \item \(\int_{0}^{1} \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^{2}}} \;dx\)
      % \item \(\int_{-1}^{1} \frac{2x}{\sqrt{1 - x^{2}}} \;dx\)
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{multicols}


  \blanklines{40}
\end{exercise}

The \hlsupp{lack of precision} in algebra manipulations, confusion from \hlsupp{misuse of notations}, and \hlsupp{not associating calculations with theorems or definitions} are the top reasons for mistakes.
