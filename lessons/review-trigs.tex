Remember that, for \(a \ge 0\), \(u^{2} \le a\) simplifies to \(-\sqrt{a} \le u \le \sqrt{a}\) and \(u^{2} \ge a\) expands to \(u \le -\sqrt{a}\) or \(u \ge \sqrt{a}\).

\[
  \sin^{-1}(\sin(x)) = x, \quad \underline{\hspace{2in}}.
\]
