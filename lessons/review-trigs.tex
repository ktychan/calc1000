Inverse trig functions undo their corresponding trig functions. You may be familiar with the identity
\[
  \sin^{-1}(\sin(x)) = x, \quad \underline{\hspace{2in}}.
\]

\faExclamationTriangle{} In \thiscourse, we denote \(\mathrm{arcsin}(x)\) by \(\sin^{-1}(x)\).
