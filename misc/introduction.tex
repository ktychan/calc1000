\textbf{Assessments and Grades}.

The course outline is an important document that outlines course policies. Think of it as a contract binding everyone in the course. All academic decisions are made in accordance with the course outline.

\begin{description}
  \item[{\hlmain{\texttt{ 8\%}}}] Homework Assignments (Best 2 out of 3)

  \item[{\hlmain{\texttt{10\%}}}] Quizzes (Best 5 out of 8)

  \item[{\hlmain{\texttt{32\%}}}] Midterm
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Friday October 30th, from 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm. See OWL for seating arrangements about a week before the midterm.
      \item Covers everything from weeks 1 to 7.
      \item The fillable course notes will make it very clear what is covered.
    \end{itemize}

  \item[{\hlmain{\texttt{50\%}}}] Final exam (cumulative)
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Cumulative means it covers every single topic in the course.
      \item \(3\) hours long.
      \item Scheduled by the Registrar's Office.
      \item Time and location will show up in your Student Centre around November (if not earlier).
    \end{itemize}

  \item[{\hlmain{\texttt{ 1\%}}}] Bonus mark for participating in research surveys (\(2\) surveys, \(0.5\%\) each)
\end{description}
